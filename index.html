Platform Detail

Where each platform stands today.

K–12 Education

Miles SchoolDesk

Digital infrastructure for modern K–12 school operations.

Miles SchoolDesk is a multi-tenant K–12 school management platform designed to give individual schools a secure digital environment to run their daily operations, under their own institutional branding. Schools coordinate students, staff, families, and finances across systems that often don't talk to each other SchoolDesk is designed to bring those workflows into one auditable, reliable platform.

Core capabilities: student records, admissions & attendance; academics, assessments & grading; fees & financial tracking; guardian access & communication; school administration tools.

Intended users: K–12 school administrators, teachers, guardians, and students within a single institution's digital environment. Status: In Development.

Higher Education

Miles Sovereign LMS

A secure digital foundation for modern universities and higher education.

Miles Sovereign LMS is a secure, multi-tenant learning and institutional management platform designed specifically for universities and higher-education institutions not a K–12 product. Universities manage academic, administrative, and financial operations at a scale and complexity that K–12 tools were never built for; Sovereign LMS is designed around institutional scale from the ground up.

Core capabilities: students, faculty & academic programs; learning management & assessments; admissions, attendance & academic records; institutional finance & permissions; institutional administration & workflows.

Intended users: university administrators, faculty, students, and institutional staff, each operating within a clearly isolated instance of the platform. Status: In Development.

Healthcare

Miles HealthOS

Building secure digital infrastructure for modern healthcare operations.

Miles HealthOS is a high-integrity, globally scalable healthcare technology platform designed to unify patient care, clinical workflows, health records, administration, and healthcare operations. AI is intended as an assistive capability only never an autonomous clinical decision-maker. Miles HealthOS does not claim medical certification, regulatory approval, clinical validation, or diagnostic accuracy at this stage, however we are working towards that.

Core capabilities: patient care & clinical workflows; health records management; healthcare administration & operations; security & interoperability.

Intended users: healthcare organizations and the clinical and administrative staff who run their day-to-day operations. Status: In Development.

Security

Miles Secure Vision System

Turning camera infrastructure into trusted situational awareness.

Miles Secure Vision System (MSVS) is a high-integrity security platform designed to transform camera networks into intelligent situational-awareness systems. MSVS does not claim guaranteed crime prevention, perfect detection, zero false positives, or any government deployment unless such claims become independently verifiable.

Core capabilities: threat detection & event validation; deterministic policies & trusted alerts; tamper-evident evidence handling; multi-tenant isolation.

Intended users: security operations teams and organizations responsible for monitoring physical premises. Status: In Development.

Commerce

Miles InstaConnect

Commerce infrastructure for businesses building their digital presence.

Miles InstaConnect is a multi-tenant commerce and marketplace platform that gives businesses their own branded digital storefront while connecting them to shared marketplace infrastructure. Miles Platforms Group provides the underlying platform infrastructure, security, tenant isolation, subscription infrastructure, and platform governance beneath each business's storefront.

Core capabilities: products, inventory & orders; customer & payment management; independent business branding; shared, governed marketplace infrastructure.

Intended users: businesses seeking a branded digital storefront, and their customers. Registration and transaction capability will be announced when the platform is available. Status: In Development.

Engineering Philosophy

Seven principles that govern how every Miles platform is built.

These aren't slogans. They are the constraints every product in our portfolio is designed against.

1

High-Integrity Engineering

Systems behave predictably under clearly defined conditions every time those conditions hold.

2

Logical Determinism

Critical operations have clearly defined, predictable, traceable outcomes.

3

Strong Isolation

One organization must never be able to see, touch, or affect another's data.

4

Auditability

Important actions are traceable who did what, when, and under what authority.

5

Resilience

Systems are designed with failure in mind, degrading gracefully rather than catastrophically.

6

Security by Architecture

Security lives in boundaries, authorization, and validation not bolted on at the end.

7

Explicit Trust Boundaries

Every system defines what is trusted, what is not, and what happens at the line between them.
